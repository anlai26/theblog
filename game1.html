MONOPOLY


Genre: Strategy / Property Trading

👥: 2 - 6 people

Duration: 1 - 3 hours

Recommended age: 8+




HOW TO PLAY?

Each player takes turns rolling the dice and moving around the board. When you land
on a property that nobody owns, you can buy it. If another player owns it, you have to
pay them rent! You can also trade properties with other players and build houses or hotels
to earn more money. The goal is to make money while trying not to go bankrupt.
The last player left in the game wins!




My Review

Monopoly is fun, especially when playing with friends. I like buying properties and
collecting rent, but sometimes the game takes so long that I just want it to end! It can
get pretty competitive, especially when someone keeps landing on your expensive
properties. Overall, it's a great game if you want something fun to play with friends or family.
