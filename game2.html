EXPLODING KITTEN


Genre: Card / Party Game

👥: 2 - 5 people

Duration: 15 - 30 minutes

Recommended age: 7+




HOW TO PLAY?

The goal is simple: don't draw an Exploding Kitten! On your turn, you can play cards
to help yourself, skip your turn, or make things harder for other players. After playing
your cards, you usually draw a card from the deck. If you draw an Exploding Kitten,
you're out of the game unless you have a Defuse card to save yourself. Keep playing
and trying to avoid the kittens until only one player remains!
